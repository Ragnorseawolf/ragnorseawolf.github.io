Privacy Policy — Plunder Player

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy describes how the app Plunder Player (the "App"), published by RagnorSeawolf ("we", "us"), handles information. Questions about this policy can be sent to ragnorseawolf@gmail.com.

Information the App itself collects

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. The App has no user accounts and no sign-in, contains no advertising and no analytics, and we operate no servers that receive your data. Your music library, playlists, settings, and listening history are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us.

Your music files

The App plays audio files stored on your device. It reads the files and their embedded tags (such as title, artist, and album art) only to display and play your library on your device. Your files are never uploaded anywhere by the App.

Wi-Fi features (Sync and Listen Here)

The App's optional Wi-Fi features connect your own devices directly to each other over your local network — for example, to sync your library between your phone and another of your devices, or to let one of your devices listen along and request track skips from another. These connections are device-to-device on your own network. Nothing is sent to us, and nothing is stored by us.

Microphone permission

The App may request microphone permission only to power its on-device audio visualizer, which reacts to the music currently playing. The App does not record audio, does not store audio, and does not transmit audio anywhere.

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play. Your payment information is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy; we never receive or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the purchased content can be unlocked on your device.

Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
