Privacy Policy — Nightmare Knight: The Throne

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy describes how the game Nightmare Knight: The Throne (the "Game"), published by RagnorSeawolf ("we", "us"), handles information. Questions about this policy can be sent to ragnorseawolf@gmail.com.

Information the Game itself collects

The Game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. The Game has no user accounts and no sign-in. Your progress, settings, and saved games are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us. We operate no servers that receive your data.

Online play

The Game offers optional online co-operative play that connects players directly to each other (peer-to-peer). To establish a connection, players exchange the network addresses needed to reach each other. This information is used only to run the game session, is not sent to us, and is not stored by us.

Advertising

The free version of the Game shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data — such as your device's advertising identifier, device information, and ad interaction data — to provide and personalize ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings. Purchasing the "Remove Ads" upgrade disables advertising in the Game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Your payment information is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy; we never receive or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the purchased content can be unlocked on your device.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
